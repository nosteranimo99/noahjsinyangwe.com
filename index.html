What I'm building · now

Golden Boot Financial

Jan 2026 — · Founder and CEO

An employer-sponsored savings platform that turns each paycheck into a personalized savings plan. Employers offer it as a benefit; employees fund it straight from payroll.

Retirement got decades of employer distribution through the 401(k). Short-term cash never did. Golden Boot routes part of each paycheck into high-yield savings, CDs, brokerage, an IRA or a custodial account, based on a short questionnaire about liquidity, horizon and risk. Assets stay with partner banks and custodians; Golden Boot is the platform and distribution layer.

I took it from idea to live launch as a solo founder, owning vision, roadmap and MVP scope. I coded the two-sided app myself (employee app and employer dashboard) in Next.js, Python/FastAPI and Plaid, with Claude Code and Codex as the engineering team, then hardened it with a software engineer: Clerk authentication, and an employer dashboard limited to aggregate reporting so no individual's finances are visible to their employer. I directed a 5-person team through a 10-week, 100-interview discovery program, ran user testing with UX designers and rebuilt onboarding around it, and own the B2B go-to-market, using Claude and Meta Muse to scale outreach to employers and employees.

38 active users · $1.5M in connected accounts · first employer live · sign-ups up 100% in a month after the onboarding redesign (Nov 2026)

FinStack IQ

Sep 2026 — · Business and Strategic Partner Lead

A financial-literacy teaching aide for middle and high schoolers, built as a game. Students create an avatar, earn virtual currency, and make choices that carry consequences into the next round.

Most financial literacy tools for kids are quizzes with a theme. FinStack IQ skips the quiz: the Hustle Block runs four missions through a consequence engine, Scamaland trains students to spot scams, and a teacher mode logs every choice as a behavioral event, so a class can be read by what students did instead of what they memorized.

I advise the founder one-on-one on business strategy and defined the target customer with the go-to-market team, which led to outreach to a first batch of teachers. I also turned the founder's vision and market insights into the working application the team now demos to investors and potential users.

Working app demoed to investors and prospective users · teacher outreach underway
